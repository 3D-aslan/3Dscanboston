# 3DScanBoston Logo Prompts

These prompts are designed to create a premium, high-tech, and professional brand identity for 3DScanBoston Studio.

---

### Style 1: The Website Integrated Mark
**Concept:** The V2 Simplified logo baked directly into the website's dark charcoal-blue grid background for a seamless look.
> **Prompt:** A professional studio logo for '3DScanBoston' featuring the 'B' icon. The 'B' is bold, half solid silver and half medium-density black wireframe. The logo is set against a very dark charcoal-blue background (#0c0c10) with a subtle, thin glowing cyan digital grid pattern and a soft teal ambient radial glow. Below the icon, the text '3DScanBoston' is rendered in a clean, modern, white sans-serif font. High-end tech aesthetic, ultra-sharp vector finish.

### Style 2: The Minimalist 2026 (Clean & Scalable)
**Concept:** A simplified version of the Studio Vision. Thicker, bolder wireframe lines with fewer polygons for maximum legibility at small sizes.
> **Prompt:** An ultra-minimalist 2D flat vector logo for '3DScanBoston'. The icon is a bold, solid block letter 'B' on a black background. The RIGHT half of the 'B' is a solid, smooth matte silver. The LEFT half is also solid silver but is overlaid with a very simple, low-density black wireframe mesh. The wireframe lines are thick, bold, and form large, clean geometric polygons. The transition between the two halves follows the curves of the 'B'. Ultra-clean design, high contrast, professional and scalable. Sans-serif text '3DScanBoston' at the bottom.

### Style 2: The Studio Vision (Final)
**Concept:** Based on the curated studio vision: A bold B where the scanning mesh is overlaid on a solid silver base. Includes brand text.
> **Prompt:** A minimalist 2D flat vector logo for '3DScanBoston'. The icon is a bold, solid letter 'B' on a black background. The RIGHT half of the 'B' is a smooth, solid matte silver with a subtle vertical sheen. The LEFT half of the 'B' is also solid silver but is overlaid with an intricate, irregular black wireframe mesh (representing 3D scan topology). The transition between the halves follows the organic curves of the letter. Below the icon, the text '3DScanBoston' is written in a clean, modern, white sans-serif font. High contrast, professional, sharp vector aesthetic.

### Style 2: The "Final" Studio Mark (Refined V3)
**Concept:** A dynamic 'B' where the scanning process (Left) meets the finished physical part (Right) with an organic, contoured split.
> **Prompt:** A premium high-tech logo for '3DScanBoston'. The icon is a bold, modern letter 'B'. The LEFT half is an intricate, glowing neon cyan CAD wireframe mesh (Fusion 360 style), showing complex geometric lines. The RIGHT half is a solid, premium matte silver metallic 3D surface. The transition between the two halves is not a straight line, but follows the organic curves and contours of the letter, creating a 'scanning in progress' effect. Vibrant cyan glow, sharp metallic detail. Isolated on a deep black background, ultra-sharp vector aesthetic, sophisticated and precise.

### 🎯 2026 Vector Styles (Simple & Scalable)
These styles are designed for modern branding: flat, minimal, and highly scalable.

#### Style 6: The Boolean Wireframe (Refined Final)
**Concept:** A solid 'B' where the left side has a technical CAD wireframe physically 'carved' out of the solid block.
> **Prompt:** A minimalist 2026 2D flat vector logo for '3DScanBoston'. A bold, geometric letter 'B'. The RIGHT half is a perfectly solid flat silver shape. The LEFT half is also solid silver, but has a complex CAD wireframe pattern physically 'booleaned' or cut out of it, revealing the black background through the stencil of the wireframe. Sharp, clean vector lines, no shadows, no gradients. Perfectly flat, high contrast. Isolated on a black background. Sophisticated engineering aesthetic.

**Concept:** A 2D flat version of the CAD split. Clean and easy to read.

The RIGHT half is a solid flat silver-gray color. No 3D effects, no shadows, no gradients. Perfectly flat design, high contrast, professional, clean whitespace. Isolated on a black background. 2026 modern tech aesthetic.

#### Style 7: The Dot Grid (Modern SaaS)
**Concept:** Uses a geometric dot pattern to represent "digital capture" in a minimal way.

The right side is a solid, flat, minimalist silver-white shape. High-precision geometric design, 2D vector style, ultra-simple, legible at small sizes. Isolated on black background.

#### Style 8: The Luxury Line-Art (Premium Tech)
**Concept:** An elegant, thin-line approach for a sophisticated studio feel.

The right half is a single clean continuous line. Minimalist, premium, single color (Cyan), transparent-ready aesthetic. Isolated on black background. 2026 branding style.
